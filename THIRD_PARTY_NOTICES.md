# Third-Party Dataset Notices

The benchmark consists of selected and reorganized third-party images. No
ownership of those source images is claimed.

## PlantVillage

- Original project: <https://github.com/spMohanty/PlantVillage-Dataset>
- Verified variant: original `raw/color` RGB images, without augmentation
- Archival release used for version identification:
  <https://doi.org/10.17632/tywbtsjrjv.1>
- Archival release license: CC0 1.0
- Citation:

  Mohanty, S. P., Hughes, D. P., and Salathé, M. (2016). Using deep learning
  for image-based plant disease detection. *Frontiers in Plant Science*, 7,
  1419. <https://doi.org/10.3389/fpls.2016.01419>

  J, Arun Pandian and Gopal, Geetharamani (2019). Data for: Identification of
  Plant Leaf Diseases Using a 9-layer Deep Convolutional Neural Network.
  Mendeley Data, V1. <https://doi.org/10.17632/tywbtsjrjv.1>

## AppleLeaf9

- Source: <https://github.com/JasonYangCode/AppleLeaf9>
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
- Citation:

  Yang, Q., Duan, S., and Wang, L. (2022). Efficient Identification of Apple
  Leaf Diseases in the Wild Using Convolutional Neural Networks. *Agronomy*,
  12(11), 2784. <https://doi.org/10.3390/agronomy12112784>

AppleLeaf9 itself combines selected images from PlantVillage, ATLDSD, Plant
Pathology 2020, and Plant Pathology 2021. Consult the source repository for its
full provenance statement.

## Paprika SAM2 fine-tuning subset

- Contents: 50 paprika leaf images, manually reviewed binary masks, and COCO
  segmentation annotations used for SAM2 fine-tuning.
- Distribution: the original data provider authorized public distribution of
  this subset through this repository as a research reproducibility resource.
- Copyright: source-image copyright remains with the original rightsholder; the
  repository-level MIT license does not relicense the source images or their
  annotations.
- Further information: see
  `sam2_finetune/Paprika_SAM2_50/DATA_USE_NOTICE.md` and
  `sam2_finetune/Paprika_SAM2_50/docs/DATA_PROVENANCE.md`.
